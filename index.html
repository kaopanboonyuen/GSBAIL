Flagship Programs

Four programs. One sovereign AI stack.

Every program is designed local-first: trained, evaluated and served inside infrastructure the bank controls, with versioning, audit trails and human oversight built in.

PROGRAM 01

KaoChat LLM

Local Intelligent Assistant · In-House Large Language Model

An intelligent conversational assistant powered by a large language model that is developed and operated inside the bank. KaoChat serves chat on GSB's own infrastructure, keeping every conversation within the organization's security boundary.

Active program
On-premises LLMThai languageBank infrastructure
PROGRAM 02

KaoOCR

Local Document Reading & Extraction

A domain-adaptive OCR framework that reads and extracts information from financial documents entirely within the organization. Built for heterogeneous layouts, Thai-language content and numerically sensitive fields, with confidence scoring and human review for uncertain results.

Active program
Domain-adaptiveLocal inferenceConfidence-aware
PROGRAM 03

Kao AI Research Laboratory

27B
Research LLM Fine-Tuning

Our core research program: investigating and fine-tuning a 27-billion-parameter language model for banking. It is the foundation for the lab's next generation of in-house models.

Active research
27B parametersFine-tuningOpen research
PROGRAM 04

Kao-LEAF Score

Financial Scoring using AI · GSB-LEAF

Learned Embeddings for Automated Feature representation. LEAF learns merchant financial behavior directly from transaction histories and keeps behavior representation, credit-outcome prediction and lending policy as separate, auditable layers.

Active program
Temporal embeddingsCredit scoringInterpretable

Principles

Three pillars, one standard.

Banking AI is judged on more than accuracy. These principles shape every model we build.

01 / TRUST

Trustworthy AI

Rigorous evaluation, fairness auditing and governance designed in from day one, not bolted on after deployment.

02 / SCALE

Scalable Systems

Architectures and infrastructure built to serve national-scale workloads reliably, from chat to transactions.

03 / CLARITY

Interpretable Intelligence

Outputs that can be explained to regulators, auditors and the customers whose financial lives depend on them.

Leadership
FOUNDING HEAD OF LAB

Dr. Teerapong Panboonyuen

ดร. ธีรพงศ์ ปานบุญยืน (ดร. เก้า)
Vice President of AI · Government Savings Bank

Dr. Teerapong ("Dr. Kao") leads GSBAIL's strategy and research direction, setting the technical and ethical standards for AI deployed across Thailand's national savings bank. He is the creator of KaoChat, KaoOCR and the GSB-LEAF scoring framework, with a focus on systems that are rigorously evaluated, transparently governed and genuinely useful at national scale.
