Our rule is simple. Sensitive conversations, documents and transaction histories are processed on bank-controlled infrastructure. No external AI service sits in the path.

How we govern it

Four programs. One sovereign AI stack.

Select a program to see how it works, from raw input to a governed output.

Trust, scale and clarity are built in, not bolted on.

Banking AI is judged on more than accuracy. Every GSBAIL system is designed so the bank can answer who controls the data, which model produced an output, and what happens when the model is unsure.

Data stays inside

Documents and conversations are processed within bank infrastructure, with no external inference endpoint required.

Every model is versioned

Parameters, training-data version, configuration and evaluation evidence are recorded together, so any output traces back to its source.

Uncertainty is routed, not hidden

KaoOCR scores its own confidence and sends uncertain reads to validation or human review instead of forcing an answer.

Drift is monitored

Production data is compared against training data. A large shift triggers review or a governed retraining, never a silent change.

People stay accountable

Our systems inform financial decisions. They do not approve credit or decide customer outcomes on their own.
