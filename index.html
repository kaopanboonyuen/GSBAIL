KaoChat LLM

Local chatbot LLM

An in-house large language model assistant that serves chat on the bank's own infrastructure. Every conversation stays inside the organization's security boundary.

02

KaoOCR

Local OCR LLM

Reads and extracts information from financial documents entirely within the organization. Built for heterogeneous layouts, Thai text and numerically sensitive fields, with confidence scoring and human review for uncertain results.

Domain-adaptiveLocal inferenceConfidence-aware

03

Kao AI Research Laboratory

Research LLM fine-tuning · 27B

Our core research program: investigating and fine-tuning a 27-billion-parameter language model for banking. The foundation for the lab's next generation of in-house models.

27B parametersFine-tuningOpen research
research
04

Kao-LEAF Score

Financial scoring using AI

Learned Embeddings for Automated Feature representation. LEAF learns merchant financial behavior from transaction histories, keeping behavior representation, credit-outcome prediction and lending policy as separate, auditable layers.
